Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

The short version

Clipbench is a desktop app that runs on your computer. We built it specifically so that your content stays with you. The app has no account system, no analytics, and no tracking.

What the app does with your data

  • Your videos never leave your machine. Transcription (Whisper) and video processing (ffmpeg) run 100% locally.
  • Transcript text is sent to Anthropic's API to find clips — using your own API key, under Anthropic's privacy terms. We never see it; the request goes directly from your machine to Anthropic.
  • Your Anthropic API key is stored locally on your machine in the app's settings file and is only ever sent to Anthropic's API.
  • License keys are checked against the Lemon Squeezy License API to activate and occasionally re-validate your purchase. That request contains your license key and a machine label — no personal files, no usage data.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. Clipbench contains no telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting. Error logs are written to a local file on your machine and are only shared with us if you choose to email them to support.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. They handle payment details, receipts, and applicable taxes under their privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details from them to provide support and license management.

Email

If you email support, we use your email to reply. That's it. No marketing lists unless you explicitly opt in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
