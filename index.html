Questions people actually ask

Why do I need my own Anthropic API key?

That's the trick that makes pay-once possible. Subscription tools mark up AI costs and charge you monthly forever. Clipbench lets you pay Anthropic directly, at cost — a full podcast episode typically costs a few cents to a few tens of cents to analyze, depending on the model you pick. Getting a key takes about two minutes, the app walks you through it, and there's a step-by-step guide here.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Transcription runs locally with Whisper (the same open-source model the pros use), and video processing is local ffmpeg. The only thing that ever leaves your machine is the transcript text, sent to the AI with your key.

What does the free trial include?

The full product — every feature, no watermark — for 3 video exports. No account, no credit card. If it earns its keep, buy a license key and keep going.

How fast is transcription?

It runs on your CPU, typically several times faster than real-time on a modern machine. A 90-minute podcast usually transcribes in roughly 10–20 minutes while you do something else — and it's free every single time.

What formats are supported?

MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, and M4V in. Clips export as 1080×1920 MP4 (H.264 + AAC) with burned-in captions, plus a text file with the post caption and hashtags.

Windows says "unrecognized app" when installing — is that normal?

Windows shows that warning for newer independent apps until they build download reputation. Click "More info → Run anyway". The installer is exactly what you downloaded from this page.
